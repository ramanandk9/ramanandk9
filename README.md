## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **PhD Researcher** at the **Human-AI Interaction (HAIx) Lab, IIT Gandhinagar**, working at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction** and **Artificial Intelligence**.

I build **multimodal systems** that make technology more intuitive, adaptive, and accessible — combining:

`👁️ Eye Tracking`&nbsp;&nbsp;`🧠 Brain-Computer Interfaces`&nbsp;&nbsp;`🗣️ Speech Recognition`&nbsp;&nbsp;`💬 NLP`&nbsp;&nbsp;`♿ Assistive Tech`&nbsp;&nbsp;`🤖 Machine Learning`

> *Research is most valuable when it leaves the lab and improves real human experiences.*
